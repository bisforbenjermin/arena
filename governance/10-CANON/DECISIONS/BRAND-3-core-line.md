# BRAND-3 — Core Line
Ruled: 2026-10-20 | Reversibility: REVERSIBLE | Undo cost: minutes | Confidence: HIGH

## Ruling
Core line is "Play is the engine. Loot is the point." It retires "a reward engine disguised as an esports platform." Mission unchanged: "Reward gamers for their time." "We know ads aren't fun. They're how we buy you more loot" is marketing and onboarding copy only, never on or beside an ad unit.

## Why
"Disguised" contradicts transparency and hands regulators their framing. Ad networks ban copy that nudges users to watch ads.

## Downstream
Every brief, C1 copy rules.
