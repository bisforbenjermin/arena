# BRAND-1 — Name Structure
Ruled: 2026-10-20 | Reversibility: EXPENSIVE once world name is public | Undo cost: rebrand | Confidence: MEDIUM (world name pending)

## Ruling
Name structure is "[World name]: Arena". The lore world is the brand; Arena is the place inside it where you compete. ARENA is the working wordmark until a world name is chosen and cleared. No brand spend before a trademark clearance search.

## Why
"Arena" is crowded in gaming (MTG Arena, Arena of Valor, Arena Breakout); a world name is ownable.

## Downstream
Wordmark, lore bible ("the Realm" placeholder), trademark search, domain.
