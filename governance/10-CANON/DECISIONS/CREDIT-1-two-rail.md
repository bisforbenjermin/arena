# CREDIT-1 — Two-Rail Credit Architecture
Ruled: 2026-07-30 | Reversibility: LOCKED | Undo cost: rewrite — the legal posture of the whole credit surface depends on it | Confidence: HIGH | Ratified by founder 2026-10-20

## Ruling
Two rails, never merged.

**Rail 1 — Keys.** Free entry access. Zero legal surface.

**Rail 2 — Vault Points.** Lore-denominated loyalty credit. Non-purchasable, non-transferable, non-cash-convertible. Carries the wedge's generosity multiplier math. Anti-speculation rules are meant to keep Rail 2 in loyalty-program territory; they aren't written yet (see Note).

## Why
Keeps the prize-chance-consideration triangle from closing on any single person, which is the core lottery-law exposure.

## Downstream
Arena Coin gate. Crowdfunding inversion structure. C2 jurisdiction switches. F2 schema. F3's eligibility gate, which is built on Rail 1 Keys.
