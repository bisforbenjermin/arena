# OPS-3 — Chat labeling convention
Ruled: 2026-08-10 | Reversibility: REVERSIBLE | Undo cost: minutes | Confidence: MEDIUM

## Ruling
Disposable chats take functional prefixes. Examples: `CLOSE v3 — F1 frozen`, `MAX — solvency ruling`, `AGENT D2`, `TROUBLESHOOT`.

## Why
Chats are disposable; the label is the only thing that makes one findable later.

## Downstream
Session hygiene. Nothing structural.
