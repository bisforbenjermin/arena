## What this is

A skill-based, venue-anchored gaming platform. Players check in at a physical venue, compete in short unpredictable micro-games, earn public rank, and win sponsor-funded prizes. Thesis: route economic value back to players from the attention economy — sponsors fund prizes in exchange for verified attention and preference data.

Status is the primary incentive. Prizes are deferred proof-of-mission. The moat is community plus an unfolding meta-narrative, not prize size.
