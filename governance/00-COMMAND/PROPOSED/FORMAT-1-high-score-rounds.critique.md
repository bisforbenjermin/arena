# Cold critique — FORMAT-1 high-score rounds

## Mechanical findings (settled by file reads)

- F1 contract already requires `durationSeconds` with explicit inclusive range **25–60** at `contracts/game-module.ts:32-33`. The proposal invents **20–120** at line 15 — a direct contradiction of a shipped field.
- G1 spec also fixes the window at **25–60s** at `specs/G1_generation_agent_brief.md:41`. The proposal silently widens both ends.
- Both reference games run at `DURATION_SECONDS = 30` (`games/precision-tap/index.ts:27`, `games/pressure-hold/index.ts:32`). They fit 25–60 and 20–120 — but they are not "already compliant" with roundTicks because the field doesn't exist.
- Precision-tap returns a non-integer score: `(this.hits_ / TARGET_COUNT) * 100` at `games/precision-tap/index.ts:136` with `TARGET_COUNT = 15`. So possible getScore values include 6.666…, 13.333…, 46.666…. Not integer. Point 2 of the proposal says "higher-is-better **integer**."
- The proposal's point 6 says "R2 converts every raw score to a rank point on a shared scale. Session leaderboards **sum** rank points." The ratified contract and R2/R3 specs talk only in **percentiles**, not a new "rank point." `contracts/scoring.ts:23,30` returns `percentile: number` (0–100). R3 spec at `specs/R3_leaderboard_rank_tokens.md:41` says "rank players by validated percentile points" — no "sum" is specified; the aggregation rule across rounds has never been ruled. The proposal invents one.
- The proposal (line 50) says it "ratifies PR-8 implicitly" and recommends PR-8 "can be withdrawn as subsumed." But FORMAT-1 point 3 declares `scoreRange: { min: integer; max: integer }` while PR-8 declared `scoreRange: { min: number; max: number }` at `PROPOSED/PR-8-score-direction-and-range.md:10`. Those are different types. "Subsumed" would silently tighten PR-8.
- Point 1 forbids "mid-round rank changes" — but R1 spec already broadcasts rounds to shared seeds (`specs/R1_orchestrator_state_machine.md:43`) and R3 is a **live leaderboard** that updates mid-round as scores validate (`specs/R3_leaderboard_rank_tokens.md:41`, "Live leaderboard: during a tournament, rank players by validated percentile points in real time"). "Mid-round rank changes" is in fact the design — the ban reads as a prohibition on R3's live view unless the author meant something else they didn't say.
- Point 7 ("multiplayer compatibility") invokes "shared-seed heat" — but CANON-2 **already** rules identical seeded rounds per bracket (PD-2, `governance/10-CANON/DECISIONS/CANON-2-board-fold.md:5`) and R1 already does this (`specs/R1_orchestrator_state_machine.md:43`). The proposal re-rules it in weaker language ("compatibility") without citing the ruling it duplicates.
- OPS-2 governs supersession: "Frozen documents are never edited in place" (`governance/10-CANON/DECISIONS/OPS-2-supersession.md:5-6`). The proposal says to version-bump the F1 contract to 2.0.0 or 3.0.0 "depending on how it merges with PR-7/PR-8" (line 34). PR-7 proposes the same 2.0.0 bump (`PROPOSED/PR-7-replayable-input.md:14`). PR-8 says "Ship it with PR-7 in contract 2.0.0" (`PROPOSED/PR-8-score-direction-and-range.md:13`). Three in-flight proposals racing for the same version number with no sequencing rule.
- `roundTicks` is redundant with `durationSeconds` under PR-2's fixed 1/60 s step (`PR-2-fixed-timestep.md:4`): roundTicks ≡ durationSeconds × 60 by definition. Adding both to GameMeta creates two sources of truth for the same quantity.
- PR-6 forbids `tilt` in the pool. The proposal says nothing about control schemes but Point 5 bans "elimination, survival, accumulation across rounds." The precision-tap sim already structurally allows the player to end early if they tap through all 15 targets ahead of tick 1800 — point 5 says that's fine ("may end early on the player's own trigger (fire, drop, finish)"). OK, but note that getScore at that early-end tick is still (hits/15)×100, so the "round length" is in fact variable. The proposal's "A round runs between 20 and 120 seconds" is therefore ambiguous: is it the budget (durationSeconds) or the realized length? Doesn't say.

## Flaws, most severe first

**1. KILL — Architect.** The proposal mandates `roundTicks` as a new required `GameMeta` field (point 4), but `durationSeconds` already exists on `GameMeta` at `contracts/game-module.ts:33` and PR-2 (`PR-2-fixed-timestep.md:4`) locks the step at 1/60 s. The two fields encode the same quantity: `roundTicks = durationSeconds × 60`. Two sources of truth for one value is exactly the kind of silent-drift surface PR-4 was ruled to prevent. **Fix:** keep `durationSeconds` (the shipped field), delete `roundTicks` from the ruling, express the bound in seconds. If the author wants ticks exposed, derive it in the runtime, not the meta.

**2. KILL — Architect.** The proposal changes the duration window from the shipped **25–60 s** (`contracts/game-module.ts:33`, `specs/G1_generation_agent_brief.md:41`) to **20–120 s** without citing or superseding the shipped bound. This is an edit-in-place of F1 and G1 by a new DECISIONS file — exactly what OPS-2 forbids (`OPS-2-supersession.md:5-6`). Also triples the max slot length, which blows up the R1 session shape (CHECK_IN → LOBBY(90s) → ROUND_1 → INTERMISSION → BONUS → ROUND_2 — `specs/R1_orchestrator_state_machine.md:41`): a 120 s round makes BONUS + two rounds + two intermissions + lobby push past five minutes. The proposal note at line 51 even admits the bound is "a value judgment, not a derivation" — then overrides a shipped, derived bound without saying so. **Fix:** either (a) keep 25–60 and say so, or (b) explicitly supersede F1's and G1's 25–60, show the R1 math for the new budget, archive the old range per OPS-2.

**3. KILL — Architect/Skeptic.** Point 6 invents a "**rank point**" and says session leaderboards **sum** them. Canon has percentiles only (`contracts/scoring.ts:23,30`, `specs/R2_score_validation_normalizer.md:44`, `specs/R3_leaderboard_rank_tokens.md:41`). No ruling and no spec says how percentiles aggregate across rounds (sum, mean, best-of, weighted). This proposal silently rules that question — "sum of percentiles" — without a Why section defending it against mean or best-of, and against the two existing rulings this would need to cite. Summing percentiles across different-shape distributions is also mathematically ugly (a player who posts 100th percentile once + 0th percentile once ties a player who posts 50/50 — probably not the designed status product). **Fix:** strike point 6, or carve it out into a separate ruling (R-ROUND-AGGREGATION) with its own options table. FORMAT-1 should name the shape of **one** round, not fold in session scoring.

**4. KILL — Architect.** Point 1 bans "mid-round rank changes." But R3 ships a **live leaderboard** that ranks "in real time" as scores validate within a round (`specs/R3_leaderboard_rank_tokens.md:41`). If "mid-round" means during one player's play of one game, say so — the current wording reads as a ban on the live view, which is the whole status product. **Fix:** rewrite point 1 as "one terminal score per round; no partial-credit emissions during a single player's play." Drop the "rank" word; rank is a cross-player quantity owned by R3.

**5. FIX — Skeptic.** Point 2 says "higher-is-better **integer**." Precision-tap's shipped `getScore` returns `(hits_/15)*100` — non-integer (`games/precision-tap/index.ts:136`). The proposal says both F4 games "already satisfy points 1–2 modulo PR-8 ratification" (line 35). They don't. Either the integer requirement is wrong, or F4 game 1 is non-compliant on day one and the proposal is lying about the undo cost ("a day today"). **Fix:** either drop "integer" (percentiles are float-friendly) or change precision-tap to return `hits_` directly (integer, range 0–15), and rewrite the proposal's "already satisfy" line.

**6. FIX — Historian/Skeptic.** The proposal says PR-8 "can be withdrawn as subsumed" (line 50). But FORMAT-1 point 3 types scoreRange as `{ min: integer; max: integer }`, PR-8 types it as `{ min: number; max: number }` (`PROPOSED/PR-8-score-direction-and-range.md:10`). "Subsumed" is false — FORMAT-1 silently tightens PR-8. The direction rule (point 2) also doesn't say what to do with a game that measures a bounded loss (e.g., ms-to-fail with a time cap): convert internally, fine, but the range for such games then has an invented ceiling. **Fix:** state that FORMAT-1 supersedes PR-8 (not subsumes), note the type-tightening, and either rule PR-8 first and cite it or move PR-8's draft to archive per OPS-2 in the same commit.

**7. FIX — Operator/Architect.** Point 7 ("multiplayer compatibility — shared-seed heat") duplicates CANON-2 rule PD-2 (identical seeded rounds per bracket, `governance/10-CANON/DECISIONS/CANON-2-board-fold.md:5`) and R1's shared-seed broadcast (`specs/R1_orchestrator_state_machine.md:43`). Ruling it again, in weaker language ("compatibility"), creates two rulings on the same fact. Also, "the orchestrator ranks within the heat and feeds the normalizer" contradicts R2's design: the normalizer ranks against (gameId, gameVersion) historical distribution per `contracts/scoring.ts:34-45`, not against a within-heat set. **Fix:** cite PD-2 instead of restating it, remove the within-heat ranking claim or carve it into a separate ruling.

**8. FIX — Operator.** The proposal describes seven interacting constraints that touch F1, F4, G1, G2, G3, G6, R1, R2, R3, PR-7, PR-8, CANON-2. "Undo cost: a day today" assumes the author can hold all those files in their head simultaneously and get it right. The ratified rulings came one at a time (PR-1..6 as six files). This is six-to-seven rulings in one atomic file; two of them (points 6, 7) are novel architecture, not standardization. **Fix:** split. One ruling per irreducible choice. Minimum split: FORMAT-1a (round shape — points 1, 2, 3, 4, 5) and FORMAT-1b (cross-round aggregation — point 6) and leave point 7 as "see PD-2 and R1."

**9. FIX — Skeptic.** The problem statement at line 6 ("the founder stated 2026-10-23 that 'high score rounds are standardized as per canon,' but no DECISIONS file carries that rule") treats the founder's one-line directive as a mandate to invent six rules. The founder's words ask for one thing: standardize what already exists in canon. The proposal exceeds that scope — it adds roundTicks, the 20-120 widening, "rank points" as a new concept, cross-round summing, elimination/survival bans, and a scoped multiplayer carve-out. The Advocate test fails: has scope crept? Yes. **Fix:** write a thin version that gathers only what's already ruled or implicit — PR-5 cold-start, PR-8 direction/range (ratified first), PD-2 shared seed, PR-2 fixed step. New rules go in their own files.
