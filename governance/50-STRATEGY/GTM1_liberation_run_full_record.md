## 1. Doctrine (why this wins)

**Viewers are free. Players are geofenced. Prizes are per-room.**

- Broadcast audience (IG Live / TikTok) scales unbounded at zero marginal prize cost.
- Prize liability scales only with venue-nights — an operator-controlled dial.
- The wedge (≤50¢-on-the-dollar sourcing) makes each room's generosity display at ~2x actual spend; breakage is margin of safety, never load-bearing.
- **HQ Trivia died because audience = players = claimants.** The venue architecture decouples those three populations. Any strategy that re-couples them is rejected on sight. This is the standing test for all future GTM proposals.

**"Prizes awarded liberally" is defined as certainty + frequency, not size.** A guaranteed witnessed win every prize night, every active venue. Approved claim shape (C1-safe): *"Someone in this room leaves with it tonight."* Commits to the event, never an individual outcome. Forbidden: "guaranteed prize," dollar-value promises to individuals, odds language.

**The "large onset" becomes a repeating onset.** Every liberation night is an onset event, scaling as the ladder climbs. Onset as franchise, not firework.

## 3. The plan

### Phase 0 — Arm the room (pre-launch)

- **Solvency model built first.** Already ledger NEXT; this decision promotes it to a hard block on the first prize night. Outputs: nightly prize floor/ceiling, cost-per-witnessed-win (CPWW) ceiling, liberation-night multiplier.
- Venue #1 secured (North County SD beachhead). Prize inventory landed via wedge. Broadcast rig tested. 2–3 local micro-creators seeded with founder status.

### Phase 1 — Fortress (weeks 1–6)

- 2–4 tournament nights/week. Ecosystem A status live; Ecosystem B direct prizes; free entry; Keys for re-entries. No points rail (gated).
- Every prize night: one guaranteed witnessed win, broadcast live. Clip pipeline: win moment cut + posted within 24h.
- Illustrative economics (the solvency model owns final numbers): $60–120 actual per night → $150–300 displayed value; ~3 nights/week ≈ $1.5–2.5k actual over 6 weeks ≈ $4–6k displayed generosity + ~20 broadcastable win moments.

### Phase 2 — Liberation ladder (the repeatable motion)

- Venue #N+1 "liberation" unlocks on a community milestone (L5 prophecy wiring: check-ins, streaks, faction counts). Pledge totals and view milestones may later join the metric set.
- Liberation night = onset event: 5–10x the nightly floor, plaque + first-on-the-board leaderboard land-grab, a creator seeded per node, fully broadcast.
- Sponsor/placement revenue attaches per node (Tier 2/3 venue offers), moving each room toward the endgame condition: placement fees > redemption costs.

### Phase 3 — Gated amplifiers (fire only when cleared)

- **Armory** (crowdfunding gate): backers arm the ladder's community vault; stretch goals = liberation milestones; backers receive status/canon/merch, never entries or eligibility. Page stays private until CLEARED.
- **Vault Points** (Arena Coin / credit gate): earn + redeem both wait on counsel under the gate's literal wording — no earn/redeem mechanic ships until CLEARED. Until then, liberality runs on direct awards + Keys only.
