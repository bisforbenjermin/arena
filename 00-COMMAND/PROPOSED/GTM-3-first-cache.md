# GTM-3 — The First Cache
Status: PROPOSED — not a ruling until the founder ratifies | Reversibility: EXPENSIVE | Undo cost: rewrite launch sequencing | Confidence: MEDIUM

## Proposal
Launch with "The First Cache" quest: free online qualifiers, city stages unlocked by milestones, then a live verified final. One player wins a home or its cash value, decided by skill. A home-builder sponsor funds it under contract before announcement. Fallback: a smaller funded prize plus the full home as an insured perfect-run bonus. Replaces the Liberation Run's Fortress start; keeps its milestone ladder and GTM-2 decoupling.
