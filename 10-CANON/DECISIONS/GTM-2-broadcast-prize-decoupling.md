# GTM-2 — Broadcast/Prize Decoupling
Ruled: 2026-07-30. Confidence: HIGH.

**Ruling:** any GTM strategy that re-couples broadcast audience size to prize cost is rejected without a full session. Standing test applied to all future GTM proposals, not a one-time evaluation criterion.

**Reversibility:** LOCKED. Undo cost: rewrite — this is the doctrine GTM-1 was selected against.

**Why:** direct lesson from HQ Trivia. When viewership drives prize liability, growth becomes the thing that kills you. Decoupling makes audience growth free.

**Downstream:** all future GTM proposals.
